** If your nrf52840 dongle is no longer pairing or working correctly even after a reflash
** get the nrf connect app from https://www.nordicsemi.com/Products/Development-tools/nRF-Connect-for-Desktop


run ./nrfconnect-5.1.0-x86_64.appimage --no-sandbox

put the dongle in dfu mode

in the connect app use the programmer option to load the mbr.hex and all_ff_fill.hex and write to the dongle

**be very carful to not overwrite the boot loader, you cannot fix it unless you sodier a debugger onto to dongle

the dongle should now be reset and you can flash OpenSK to it normally
